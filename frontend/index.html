About ShopAgent

ShopAgent is a rule-grounded footwear-shopping agent that converts natural-language requests into explicit hard constraints, filters products using those constraints, and explains recommendations using catalogue facts. No LLM. No external APIs. No stored queries.

Dataset & honest limitations

Prices are a May 2023 snapshot and are not live. Colour is known for approximately 66% of products and gender for approximately 89%; unknown values never satisfy a requested constraint. No size or live-stock fields — products are catalogue listings, not confirmed inventory. Ratings exist for about 38% of products. Men’s footwear is under-represented. Product images and listing links belong to Myntra; this is an academic, non-commercial demo.
